At a glance
  1. 1 · WhatA fair head-to-head of 15 forecasting methods on transformer oil temperature, 24 hours ahead, including three zero-shot foundation models.
  2. 2 · WhyTeams must choose between fast statistics, machine learning and new pretrained models; this repo's first benchmark was not like-for-like.
  3. 3 · WhoUsers: forecasting and data science teams in utilities and operations.Data: ETTh1 (Informer, AAAI 2021): 17,420 hourly readings of oil temperature and six loads, July 2016 to June 2018.
  4. 4 · WhereResults page and technical walkthrough on GitHub Pages; dashboard on Streamlit Community Cloud, leading with the fair benchmark.
  5. 5 · When it failsPass mark fixed before any run: the zero-shot champion must beat the trained champion by ≥ 5%, pass a significance test, and win on the last 41 days.No live A/B test: an offline rolling test of 145 daily forecasts. Found on the way: the old benchmark fed ML the true past values.
  6. 6 · HowEvery model forecasts 24 hours from the same history at 145 start points; champions picked on validation; Diebold–Mariano test.Result: zero-shot Chronos-2 misses by 1.13 °C on average, 19% less than the best trained model, Holt-Winters (1.40 °C).
